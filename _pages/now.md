<div class="now-prose">
  <section><h2>Work</h2><p>I am a Compiler Engineer in the Compiler Team at Hyundai Motor Company. My work involves TVM-based compilation for custom NPUs, including lowering, scheduling, and memory mapping.</p><p>I completed my Ph.D. at Yonsei University in August 2026, advised by Prof. Dosik Hwang.</p></section>
  <section><h2>Research</h2><p>Test-time adaptation remains my main research interest. I am interested in what a model can safely learn from incoming, unlabeled data, and how that affects its predictions under distribution shift.</p><p>Medical image segmentation and reconstruction are recurring applications. Efficient deployment is another part of the same question: how to make a model useful when its data and execution environment differ from the conditions in which it was trained.</p></section>
  <section><h2>Reading &amp; building</h2><ul><li>Adaptation mechanisms that change a small part of a pretrained model.</li><li>Tensor programs, memory reuse, and the path from Relax to TIR.</li><li>Small experiments that make these mechanisms easier to inspect.</li></ul><p>Some of these ideas are collected in the <a href="{{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}">research notes</a> and <a href="{{ '/lab/' | relative_url }}">interactive lab</a>.</p></section>
