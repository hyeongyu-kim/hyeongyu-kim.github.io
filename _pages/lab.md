    <p>45 recorded PyTorch experiments share the RobustBench Standard CIFAR-10 WRN-28-10 checkpoint, fixed image indices, and mean prediction entropy. Adam: β=(0.9, 0.999), ε=10⁻⁸, no weight decay. Seed: 1. At batch 16/128, learning rates are 0.001 for Tent/Buffer and 0.01 for AcTTA. At batch 4, they are 0.0001 for Tent, 0.001 for Buffer, and 0.001 for AcTTA. Batch 16 uses the public batch-128 learning rates as an explicit demo override. Inputs are unnormalized [0,1] RGB from the first 512 original test images. All outcomes are retained. Labels never enter the update.</p>
    <p>Tent updates every BN affine parameter. Buffer uses its public WRN model: a branch after the initial convolution plus independent branches after both ReLUs in each block of stages one and two. α starts at 10⁻⁵; trainable mixing coefficients start at 0.5. AcTTA uses the released activation and entropy adapter, replacing the eight independent ReLUs in stage one with channel-wise c, λ+, λ− and fixed β=1. It freezes BN affine parameters. No activation sites share adaptation parameters. All ineligible backbone weights are checked for bitwise equality after every update.</p>
    <p>The one-batch experiment records 0–8 repeated updates to the same unlabeled batch. The stream has four batches per phase: clean → Gaussian noise (severity 3) → defocus blur (severity 5) → clean. The final clean phase reuses the original indices. Each incoming batch gets one update. Predictions, internal states, and the table show the current batch <strong>after</strong> its update. Stream history shows predictions <strong>before</strong> that batch's update. Reset restores both the parameters and optimizer.</p>
    <p>Accuracy, entropy (nats), and ECE (ten equal-width confidence bins) use every image in the batch. Feature maps inspect channels 0–15 at block1.layer.0.relu1 (16 channels) and block1.layer.1.relu1 (160 channels), pool them to 8×8, and quantize them for display with a shared signed scale across methods and steps. Buffer maps include the correction at the matching site. Buffer's smaller residual has its own scale. Probabilities and parameters keep decimal precision.</p>
    <p>Public model, activation, placement, and optimizer configurations are used here. These fixed-order subsets and repeated updates remain a mechanism demonstration, rather than a reproduction of the full shuffled paper benchmarks or a ranking of methods. Repeated-batch results concern the adapted inputs, not unseen-data generalization. Entropy can fall while accuracy gets worse; small batches make accuracy and calibration estimates noisy.</p>
