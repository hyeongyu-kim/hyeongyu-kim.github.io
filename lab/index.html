Protocol, measurements, and reproducibility

45 recorded PyTorch experiments share a pretrained CIFAR-10 ResNet-20 checkpoint, fixed image indices, mean prediction entropy, and Adam: learning rate 0.001, β=(0.9, 0.999), ε=10⁻⁸, no weight decay. Seed: 27. Inputs are the first 256 test images from the original CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-10-C releases. All outcomes are retained. Labels never enter the update.

Tent updates every BN affine parameter. Buffer inserts ReLU + α(s₁·Conv1×1 + s₃·Conv3×3) at the stem and stage one, with α initialized to 10⁻⁵ and trainable mixing coefficients initialized to 0.5. AcTTA uses Eq. 1 of its paper with a ReLU base, per-channel c, λ+, λ−, and fixed sigmoid temperature β=1. It is inserted at the stem, stage one, and the first stage-two block. Shared activations share their parameters. All ineligible backbone weights are checked for bitwise equality after every update.

The one-batch experiment records 0–8 repeated updates to the same unlabeled batch. The stream has four batches per phase: clean → Gaussian noise (severity 3) → defocus blur (severity 5) → clean. The final clean phase reuses the original indices. Each incoming batch gets one update. Predictions, internal states, and the table show the current batch after its update. Stream history shows predictions before that batch's update. Reset restores both the parameters and optimizer.

Accuracy, entropy (nats), and ECE (ten equal-width confidence bins) use every image in the batch. Feature maps capture the first call to each inspected activation, pool it to 8×8, and quantize it for display with a shared signed scale across methods and steps. Buffer's smaller residual has its own scale. Probabilities and parameters keep decimal precision.

This is a controlled demonstration of update mechanisms, not a reproduction of the papers' full benchmarks or a ranking of methods. Repeated-batch results concern the adapted inputs, not unseen-data generalization. Entropy can fall while accuracy gets worse; small batches make accuracy and calibration estimates noisy.
